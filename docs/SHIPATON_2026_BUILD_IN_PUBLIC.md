## Post 1 — the negative result

> We just made a product decision I wish more AI projects showed publicly.
>
> Our PAUL Open DPO V6 checkpoint improved a matched automated diagnostic (90.62 vs 89.18), but a fresh 12-case development comparison preferred the SFT reference on 6 cases vs V6 on 3.
>
> So we did **not** promote V6.
>
> For #Shipaton, that changed the app: the Research tab now shows the evidence boundary instead of a "new model = better model" claim.
>
> Building PAUL Open in public means publishing the failures too. #BuildInPublic

## Post 2 — product scope

> Cut scope today.
>
> PAUL Open for #Shipaton now has one job: help people understand science more rigorously across languages.
>
> 3 modes:
> • Explain
> • Tutor me
> • Research lens
>
> First languages:
> • English
> • Hindi
> • Bengali
>
> No giant "AI everything" dashboard. Fewer features, clearer learning loop. #BuildInPublic

## Post 3 — RevenueCat integration

> RevenueCat isn't a checkbox in our #Shipaton build.
>
> Free stays useful: Explain + Tutor, 5 sessions/day.
>
> Pro unlocks:
> • evidence-first Research lens
> • 40 sessions/day
> • native subscription management
>
> Paywall, entitlement state, restore and Customer Center are all handled through RevenueCat.
>
> Localized price stays in the store UI instead of being hard-coded into the app. #BuildInPublic

## Post 4 — trust boundary

> A small architecture choice that matters:
>
> Our mobile app never ships the Gemini API key.
>
> Capacitor app → small server-side API → Gemini
>
> And the production runtime is explicitly separate from the PAUL Open experimental checkpoints. The repo is the research source of truth; the app does not silently turn a research result into a marketing claim.
>
> #Shipaton #BuildInPublic

# Shipaton 2026 — 2-minute demo script

Target length: **115–120 seconds**. Record the real Android build; do not use a Figma-only mockup.

## 0:00–0:12 — Problem

Screen: app launch → Learn.

Voice:

> Science answers are easy to generate. Reliable understanding is harder — especially across languages. PAUL Open is a multilingual science companion built around explanation, questioning and evidence.

## 0:12–0:38 — Explain

Screen: choose English → Explain → ask a syringe/Boyle's-law question.

Voice:

> In Explain mode, the app focuses on the mechanism, assumptions and common misconceptions — not just a final answer.

Show the generated response for several seconds.

## 0:38–0:58 — Tutor + multilingual

Screen: switch to Hindi or Bengali → Tutor me → use a quick prompt.

Voice:

> Tutor mode starts with a diagnostic question and then guides the learner. The first release supports English, Hindi and Bengali while keeping scientific notation readable.

## 0:58–1:18 — Research lens

Screen: tap Research lens; if on Free, show Pro gate, then show the Pro-enabled flow in the judge build.

Voice:

> Pro adds an evidence-first Research lens: distinguish observation from inference, consider confounders, and propose a practical way to test the claim.

## 1:18–1:35 — RevenueCat

Screen: Pro tab → RevenueCat Paywall → close → Restore / Customer Center.

Voice:

> RevenueCat is the product's access layer. The free tier stays useful, while Pro unlocks Research lens and more daily learning sessions. RevenueCat handles the native paywall, entitlements, restoration and subscription management.

## 1:35–1:52 — Research transparency

Screen: Research tab.

Voice:

> We also show our research boundary. One experimental checkpoint improved an automated score but did not pass our fresh development comparison, so we did not promote it. The app reads this status from the public repository instead of hard-coding a marketing claim.

## 1:52–2:00 — Close

Screen: app hero + logo.

Voice:

> PAUL Open: understand the science, question the evidence, and learn in your language.

## Recording gate

Before recording:

- real Android build;
- real RevenueCat offering visible;
- at least one successful sandbox purchase/entitlement test;
- production/demo AI endpoint online;
- no API keys, console tokens, email addresses or admin dashboards visible;
- video <= 2:00 after final export.

# PAUL Open — Google Play listing draft

## App name

PAUL Open

## Short description

Learn science through clear explanations, guided tutoring and evidence-first thinking.

## Full description

PAUL Open is a multilingual science-learning companion built to help you understand ideas instead of only collecting answers.

**Explain**
Get a concise explanation of the key mechanism, assumptions and common misconceptions.

**Tutor me**
Start with a focused diagnostic question and work toward understanding with guided support.

**Research lens — Pro**
Separate observation from inference, consider alternative explanations and confounders, and build a practical validation plan.

**Languages**
The first release supports English, Hindi and Bengali, while preserving standard scientific notation where it improves clarity.

**Transparent research**
PAUL Open is also an open research initiative. The app's Research screen reads current status from the versioned public repository, including negative results and limitations. Experimental checkpoints are not automatically treated as production models.

PAUL Open uses AI and can make mistakes. Verify important scientific, medical, safety-critical or real-world decisions using appropriate sources or qualified expertise.

Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Google Play. Prices are localized and displayed before purchase.

## Privacy-policy draft points for website counsel/review

- No account/email is required by the current MVP client.
- Learning prompts are transmitted to the PAUL Open Companion backend and configured AI provider to generate responses.
- The MVP backend is designed not to log prompt/response text intentionally; operational metadata may include request ID, mode, language and latency.
- Google Play / RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information under their respective terms.
- The app fetches public research-status data from GitHub.
- Do not promise zero provider retention unless the deployed provider/account configuration actually guarantees it.

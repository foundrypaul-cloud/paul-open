# PAUL Open Model Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the PAUL Open research initiative a harassment-free experience for everyone. We explicitly support researchers, developers, educators, students, technical contributors, and interdisciplinary contributors.

We are committed to building an open, AI-first environment that encourages rigorous technical discussion, respectful disagreement, evidence-based criticism, reproducible research, transparent attribution, and constructive peer review.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our research community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints, methodologies, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive peer review
* Focusing on what is best for the research, reproducibility, and scientific integrity
* Clearly distinguishing between validated results and hypotheses

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
* Manipulating training data, evaluations, or benchmarks to misrepresent model performance

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub moderation tools or established project contact points.
